I work in gravitational-wave physics, with interests in gravitational-wave data analysis, Bayesian parameter estimation, template-bank construction, tests of general relativity, and next-generation gravitational-wave detectors.

Research

My research focuses on methods for extracting astrophysical and fundamental-physics information from gravitational-wave observations. Current interests include fast Bayesian inference, long-duration signals in third-generation detectors, searches for compact binaries, and tests of general relativity.
